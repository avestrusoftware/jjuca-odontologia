## Prompts de geração anteriores

### Ipanema

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square photographic postcard-style background for a premium dental website's experience card. Primary request: a beautiful geographically faithful view of Ipanema beach in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, viewed from the Arpoador rocks looking west toward the distinctive two rounded peaks of Morro Dois Irmaos. Recognizable sweeping Ipanema shoreline, warm golden sand, gentle seafoam waves, blue-green Atlantic water, lush green hills, elegant warm late-afternoon light and thin atmospheric haze. Editorial travel photography, realistic natural color and fine details, calm welcoming feeling. Foreground rock texture along a small bottom corner, mountains prominent in upper central third, ocean and beach across the middle and lower field. Square framing suited to a 420px website card with text overlays added separately in HTML: preserve simple darker softly lit regions near the top left and bottom left, no artificial gradient baked in. This is a scenic illustrative visual of the neighborhood, not a photograph of the clinic. No lettering, no postcard border, no logo, no watermark, no giant crowds, no invented landmarks or buildings. No Christ statue, no Sugarloaf inserted in an incorrect view. Refined muted green and warm golden sunset tones.

### Cena do scanner

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square photographic background for an intraoral scanner technology card on a premium dental clinic website. Primary request: close-up over-the-shoulder scene of a dental professional using an optical intraoral scanner with a computer-based 3D dental scan application. Large modern monitor showing a believable 3D upper and lower dental arch model in ivory and pale cyan on a muted dark sage/forest green CAD interface, minimal fine scan controls and small non-legible UI marks. A correctly formed hand wearing a white medical glove holds a slim white intraoral scanning wand at the lower right, oriented toward an out-of-focus reclining patient outside the lower frame; no identifiable patient face, no procedural gore. A small part of a clinician shoulder at right edge, monitor and technology are the focus. Contemporary real dental workspace, subtle sage wall and warm wood, natural soft window light, shallow depth of field, editorial healthcare photography, very high realism. Compose the monitor as the middle background layer; the center-right will later receive a separate floating tooth cutout in HTML, so do NOT add any floating tooth in the photo. Upper left and lower left remain visually quiet for website text added separately. No branded devices, no brand logos, no readable slogans, no watermark, no synthetic sci-fi interface, no exaggerated neon. The scene is illustrative, not a documentary image of a specific clinic or team.

### Dente natural

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a transparent foreground cutout for a premium dental website card, layered over an intraoral scanning photograph. Primary request: ONE complete natural human molar tooth, photorealistic high-end dental product rendering. A beautiful glossy ivory-white enamel crown, realistic subtle translucent cusp edges, fine occlusal grooves, natural slightly warm root surface and two gracefully tapered natural roots. NOT an implant: absolutely no metal, screw, abutment, dental hardware, base, stand or gum tissue. Three-quarter view with the biting surface visible, tooth tilted approximately 12 degrees clockwise, fully visible, centered, generous transparent margin. Large crown, elegant compact proportions, soft studio lighting with gentle sage-green reflected rim light and clean highlights. Real material texture without exaggerated wet shine. Isolated single tooth on a genuinely transparent alpha background, no floor, no cast background, no lettering, no frame, no watermark. White premium dental enamel, not cartoon, not a flat vector, not a wireframe.
